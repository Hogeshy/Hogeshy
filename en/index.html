A quiet place to stop

Hogeshy

A cat to spend time with when you do not want to do anything.

For people who are a little tired of trying hard, and a little tired of being told to heal.

Hogeshy does not give you energy or answers. It simply stays beside you.

You do not have to be productive, cheerful, or certain here. You can spend a little time doing nothing.

Hogeshy is a hand-drawn black cat-like creature that eats emptiness, eats canned cat food, and thinks about nothing.

Maybe that is happiness.
