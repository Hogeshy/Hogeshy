What is
Hogeshy?

A black cat born from a feeling of looseness and a feeling of emptiness.

The official explanation

Hogeshy is a black cat born from Hoge, a feeling of looseness, and Munashy, from munashii, meaning empty.

Hogeshy eats nothingness. Hogeshy eats canned cat food. Hogeshy thinks about nothing.

But, probably, Hogeshy is happy.

Hogeshy is always looking for something.

Happiness. Misfortune. Nothingness. Canned cat food. Or something else.

Hogeshy is not entirely sure.

Hogeshy is not trying to tell humans anything.

Hogeshy is simply living, every day.

Here, there are short stories, daily records, and a few small comics.

None of them are particularly important. Probably.

Still, every day, Hogeshy keeps living.
