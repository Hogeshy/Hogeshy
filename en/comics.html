Hogeshy’s comics

One-page comics

Small, quiet stories. Choose one and take your time.

Beside the ShadowThe shadow followed a little late.

Hogeshy stopped.
The shadow stopped too.
They watched each other for a while.
Hogeshy did not chase it away.

Hogeshy simply sat beside it.
The Other Side of the RainIt felt as if someone was on the other side of the rain.

Hogeshy did not open the window.
It simply sat on this side of the rain
for a while.
The BoxHogeshy opened the box.

There was another box inside.

Hogeshy did not open it any further.
The StringHogeshy pulled the string.

Nothing moved on the other side.

Hogeshy stopped pulling
and waited.
The Warm SpotHogeshy found a warm place.

The light moved little by little.

Hogeshy moved a little too.

Without doing anything.
The DoorThere seemed to be something beyond the door.

Hogeshy did not open it.

It simply sat in front of it.
The MoonThe moon was there.

Hogeshy looked up at it
for a while.

That was all.
A Small ChairOne small chair had appeared in the usual room.

Hogeshy did not sit on it.

It simply stayed beside it.
The NotificationA notification arrived.
Hogeshy did not look at it right away.
It turned the screen face down
and faced the quiet instead.
Today, that was enough.
The Day It Could Not Be SeenWhat Hogeshy wanted to see
was not there.

Hogeshy was disappointed.

Still, it went home while disappointed.
It did not have to turn the disappointment
into a good memory.
