About Hogeshy

What is Hogeshy?

For people who are a little tired of trying hard, and a little tired of being told to heal. Hogeshy does not give you energy or answers. It simply stays quietly beside you.

Hogeshy was born while its creator was taking time away from work.

There were days when nothing felt worth doing, and nothing felt fulfilling. The body felt empty. The heart felt as if a small hole had opened inside it.

While looking quietly at those feelings, Hogeshy appeared in the shape of a beloved black cat.

There is one eye at the end of Hogeshy’s tail. It is an eye for looking at yourself from a little distance—not to judge, but to notice when you are pushing too hard.

Hogeshy also has days when it does not want to think, move, or do anything. Still, somewhere inside, it wants to be happy.

So Hogeshy does not try to cheer anyone up. It does not offer the right answer or a positive phrase. It simply stays beside you, spending time with you while doing nothing.

If Hogeshy ever looks lonely, perhaps you are seeing a little of your own loneliness. You do not need to find an answer. You can simply notice how you feel.
