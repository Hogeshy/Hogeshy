Cookie Policy

We define the use of cookies and related technologies on this site regarding the provision of better content, improvement of user experience, and analysis of usage status as follows.

On this site, we use cookies and similar technologies for the purpose of providing better content, improving user experience, and analyzing usage status.

1. Purpose of Cookie Usage

We use cookies on this site for the following purposes:

  • Analysis of site usage status and traffic
  • Improvement of experience through the analysis of user browsing behavior (such as scrolling and interactions)
  • Maintenance and optimization of security

2. External Analytics and Analysis Tools Used

We use the following external tools to collect and process data on this site:

  • Google Analytics (GA4)
    We use Google Analytics provided by Google to measure access status and analyze traffic. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect user website usage data. The collected data is managed based on Google's privacy policy.
    For details and how to disable it, please check the Google Policy and Terms and the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
  • Microsoft Clarity
    We use Microsoft Clarity, an access analysis tool provided by Microsoft, for the purpose of improving user experience. Clarity anonymously collects and analyzes behavioral data such as clicks, scrolls, and dwell time, and utilizes it for site improvement. In addition, operation contents during page browsing may be recorded anonymously as session recordings, but information that can identify individuals, such as names and addresses, is not collected. The collected data is appropriately managed based on Microsoft's terms of use and privacy policy.
    For details regarding Microsoft Clarity, please refer to the Microsoft Privacy Statement.
  • Google Search Console
    We use Google Search Console to analyze the display status, search performance, and technical errors of our site in search engines. Google Search Console does not directly use cookies, but it communicates with Google's servers.

3. Management and Disabling of Cookies

You can choose or restrict the acceptance of cookies by changing your browser settings. Please note that changing your settings may cause some services to not function properly.

You can check the methods for setting cookies in major browsers from the following official links:

  • Google Chrome
  • Microsoft Edge
  • Safari (Mac)
