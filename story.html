HOGESHY
ホーム 物語ホゲシーとは 日記 無料 ギャラリー お店 インスタグラム

最初の40日間

静かな探しもの。

ホゲシーは空っぽを食べ、 猫缶を食べ、 何も考えない。 でも、たぶんしあわせ。

[Day 1]
いつもと変わらない一日。

[Day 2]
Nothing special happened today.
Just me, cat food,
and a strange sense of peace.
I think that's enough.

It’s night again.
I ate some cat food and watched the void for a while.
The city was still awake.
I wasn’t. Maybe tomorrow will be different. ...probably not.

[Day 4]

[Day 32]

It was.
33日目
[Day 33]

35日目
[Day 35]

36日目
[Day 36]

It was probably tired.
37日目
[Day 37]

On the other side,
Hogeshy was looking at it.
38日目
[Day 38]
There was a light outside.

Hogeshy moved closer.

The light moved away.
39日目
[Day 39]
Hogeshy was looking outside.

The tail was not.

It was looking back.
40日目
[Day 40]
Hogeshy had been looking for something.

The can was there.
The box was there.
The key was there.

Hogeshy stopped looking.
